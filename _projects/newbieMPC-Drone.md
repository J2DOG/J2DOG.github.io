Simple quadrotor flight featuring a nonlinear MPC controller based on quadrotor dynamics. It demonstrates trajectory optimization and obstacle avoidance by incorporating obstacle penalties into the cost function, with obstacles modeled as elliptic cylinders. The 12-DOF model includes position, orientation, linear and angular velocities; the controller is tuned via `myMPController.m` and `mySetup.m`.
